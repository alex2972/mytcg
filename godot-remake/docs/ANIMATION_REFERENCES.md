# Original video references

Battle pacing is editable in `ui/battle_timing.gd`. Values are seconds: `*_DURATION` changes animation speed, while `*_PAUSE` adds a wait between stages. Save and restart the duel after editing. The default values preserve the current presentation; response effects and LP animations still finish before input unlocks. Explosion-stage Graveyard handoff scales automatically with destruction duration.

Opponent hand cards are arranged newest on the left. Each draw inserts at the left edge while older cards spread smoothly to the right, preserving the inverted resting orientation. Batched effect draws grow the displayed hand one card at a time; future draws stay hidden until their own flight.

The eight clips in `Z:/process/videos` are the reference for these presentations. They were recorded at 10 fps: stage boundaries have approximately 0.1 second uncertainty. Playback uses time-based interpolation and the extracted original PNGs, without reproducing capture compression or dropped frames. Presentation completes before the engine acknowledges the next operation. AI and card rules were not changed.
